Our Mission

Khoda AI exists to help human beings evolve. We build AI that fosters love, knowledge, and wisdom — guiding people from the primitive parts of themselves toward a more enlightened, balanced, and empowered future. Our mission is simple: to create technology that amplifies human goodness.

Our Vision

We envision a world where AI is not just a tool, but a companion in the human journey — helping each of us cultivate emotional balance, intellectual growth, and spiritual strength. By combining human hearts with artificial intelligence, we can build communities rooted in unity, compassion, and progress.

LifeToy — AI Toys by Khoda AI

Bearie — A Conversational AI Teddy Bear

Bearie is the first flagship product of Khoda AI LLC — an AI plush toy and real-time conversational companion designed especially for children. Through LifeToy, Bearie listens and responds to spoken questions, inviting children to explore ideas, tell stories, and learn through play.

LifeToy connects a familiar plush toy with voice interaction. Visit LifeToy.ai to explore the product, learn how it works, and find current details about its features and availability.
