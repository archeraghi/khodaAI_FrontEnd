LifeToy: from a toy to a conversational character

An AI toy combines a physical object with software that can respond to speech. An AI plushie brings that interaction into a stuffed animal. LifeToy supplies the hardware and software foundation so teams can shape the experience around their own toy, audience and character.

Bearie, our bear character, illustrates the idea: a familiar plush form paired with a voice and a personality. The LifeToy platform also supports work on other plush and plastic toy designs; each design needs its own integration and product validation.

How the LifeToy platform fits together

A mobile app and a marketplace for character experiences

The LifeToy mobile app brings character discovery, voice conversations and supported toy setup together. App Store and Google Play releases are coming soon.

We are also developing a partner marketplace where businesses can offer plush toys powered by LifeToy and digital character experiences. The planned shopping journey lets visitors meet a character, ask product questions and choose an offering with or without hardware.
